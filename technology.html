The streams can only be combined when image and genomic records correspond to the same eligible participants or properly matched cohorts. Otherwise, they must be analyzed separately and their limitations reported.

Image-analysis stream

A computer-vision model could be evaluated for image quality assessment or retinal feature extraction, depending on the annotations available. A fundus photograph alone does not directly measure RNFL thickness; that generally requires an appropriate modality such as optical coherence tomography (OCT).

PythonTensorFlow / KerasOpenCV

Genomic-analysis stream

Variant interpretation requires careful annotation, evidence review and an understanding of inheritance and clinical context. A gene list is not equivalent to individual genomic variant data and cannot independently produce a participant-level risk prediction.

SFARI GeneVariant annotationBioinformatics

Model evaluation

For a valid supervised model, keep participants—not merely images—in separate training, validation and test groups to reduce data leakage. Report the class balance and compare against suitable baseline models.

AccuracyOverall proportion classified correctly
SensitivityProportion of positives detected
ROC-AUCDiscrimination across thresholds

Also report precision, specificity, F1-score, confidence intervals and limitations. No performance value should be published until it has been measured on a held-out test set.
